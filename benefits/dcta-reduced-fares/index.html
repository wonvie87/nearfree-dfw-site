DCTA Reduced FaresDiscounts for seniors, disability, Medicare and students

Eligible seniors, riders with disabilities, Medicare recipients, and students age 6-18 can apply for reduced DCTA fares.

THE EXPERIENCE

What to expect

Eligible riders first obtain a Reduced Fare Eligible badge. The badge is not a ticket, so riders still buy the reduced fare or pass for the service they use; prices vary by service.
