BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Questions, answered plainly.

Can I create an account today?

Not yet. The buttons show where sign-in and membership will live, but this version does not collect account or billing information.

Will clicking Join Radar charge me?

No. It opens a clear product-status notice. Real billing will be added only with a secure backend and full account controls.

Will free users lose listing details?

No. Verified price, date, place, eligibility, booking conditions and source links remain available to everyone.

What is planned for cancellation?

The product is planned as a monthly subscription with self-service cancellation. Final terms will be shown before any real purchase.

What can I try now?

You can build a Radar preview, view real current matches and save those preferences on this device without an account.
