Family Fright Fest

Stewart Creek Park hosts hay rides, games, trick-or-treat booths, crafts, and age-group costume contests, with event activities free to join.

THE EXPERIENCE

What to expect

From 3 to 7 PM, Stewart Creek Park hosts hay rides, games, crafts, and trick-or-treat booths. Costume contests are divided by age, from ages 0-3 through 13 and older.
